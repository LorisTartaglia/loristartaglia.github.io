ABOUT ME

A life dedicated to listening to the vine and its silences

Wine has never been just a profession for me, but a language to decode the world. My story originates from the land, from observing natural cycles, and from the realization that every soil has a unique voice waiting to be heard and understood. My educational background started with technical and agronomic foundations, but found its true calling when theory met the hard work and magic of the harvest.

A Global Experience

To fully understand the potential of the vine, I felt the need to step beyond traditional boundaries. From the extreme climates of the Southern Hemisphere to the historic cellars of the Old Continent, I had the privilege of engaging with profoundly diverse production philosophies. This total immersion in contrasting terroirs shaped my sensitivity, teaching me the art of adaptability: not imposing a predetermined method, but tailoring techniques to the specific needs of the grapes and the vintage.

"The winemaker's role is not to dominate the matter, but to guide it towards its ultimate expression, preserving the soul of the terroir."

Choice and Style

Today, my oenological vision is built on three pillars: respect, purity, and identity. I firmly believe in a cellar approach that is as respectful as possible, where technique acts as a shield to protect the integrity of the fruit, never as a mask to alter its traits. My stylistic choice rejects standardization: I seek wines that display tension, elegance, and a vibrant adherence to their terroir.

Collaborating with estates means entering their history with profound respect, analyzing their untapped potential, and translating it into wines capable of stirring emotions, defying time, and telling the uncompromising story of their unique origins.
